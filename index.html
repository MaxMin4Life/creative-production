The Bazaar

My involvement: Mar 2025 - Aug 2025

  • Led production of the live PC/Mac game while owning the backlog for PC modernization, the mobile build, and migration from Unity 2022 to Unity 6. Coordinated shared architecture, data-driven systems, plugins, dependencies, and release work across Steam, iOS, and Android initiatives within a 95-person international team.
  • Coordinated engineering, art, and technical-art work on responsive UI layouts and modular startup and asset-loading systems, supporting mobile adaptation and faster content iteration.
  • Led work toward AAA standards of performance and usability, building on the game's established art quality and connecting those goals to development priorities and release readiness.
  • Took over the DevOps team and refocused its roadmap on immediate product and release requirements. Produced a master release checklist that made the remaining work explicit and became the basis for prioritizing automation.
  • Reworked feature scoping from up to four days of meetings into a shared feature document completed and reviewed asynchronously by Design, Engineering, Art, Audio, and other disciplines. Estimates, dependencies, and technical questions surfaced before formal review; the final scope and sign-off meeting often took under an hour.
  • Built production and QA dashboards to make testing status, blockers, milestones, and release risk visible, supporting decisions across the live game and parallel platform initiatives.
